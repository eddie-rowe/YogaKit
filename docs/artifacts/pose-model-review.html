what actually changes
derived= Base of support: conserved. Hands + feet before and after — nothing added or removed. The dominant reason this feels effortless.
derived↑ COG rises & travels back over the hips — one clean vector, no scatter.
derived→ Joints move monotonically: hips neutral→flexion, shoulders 90°→overhead. Same direction, nothing fights.
derived= Spine stays neutral — continuation, not reversal.

Low because base of support is conserved, there's no prone↔supine flip, the COG follows a single vector, and every joint change is monotonic. The sequencer prefers low-friction edges — and can flag a high-friction one as intentional (a deliberate rebound) rather than an accident.

The generalizable rule

A transition flows when the base of support and orientation are conserved, and only the center of gravity plus a few joint angles change along one coherent vector. It's the same magic in chaturanga → up dog → down dog: hands and feet stay planted, the body just arcs over a fixed base.
