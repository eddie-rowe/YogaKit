A Worked Example

The four silences

Not a chapter of the book — a demonstration of the kind of call this guide is asking for. Four points in a class where nothing is supposed to happen, taught almost interchangeably. The measurements sort them; only a teacher can say what to do with the sort.

Four ways to ask a body to do nothing

Child's Pose, Constructive Rest, Seated Stillness, Savasana — four rests, timed the same, rated the same easy intensity.

What they're not — the same rest. Constructive Rest and Savasana are close cousins, both already let go of upright. Child's Pose and Seated Stillness look like their upright counterparts, but don't measure as a matching pair. The four sort as one true pair and two poses that only resemble one.

How To Respond

No format required

Margins are fine

Notes on a printout, a voice memo, a marked-up PDF — whatever is fastest for you. Nothing here needs to arrive as prose.

Answer out of order

The questions are grouped by part, not by priority. Start wherever you have the strongest opinion already formed.

"I don't know yet" counts

Several of these took the library sixty-seven poses to even notice. An honest unknown is more useful here than a guess.
