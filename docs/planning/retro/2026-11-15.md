- **A ratchet has to check content, not count.** "Exactly N" blocks the shrink. "At most N" allows a refill. Check membership against a frozen baseline.

The rest parsed as code, tests stayed green and the gate exited 0. It lasted one push on #304's branch and never reached `main`. `eslint` warnings do not fail the local gate.
