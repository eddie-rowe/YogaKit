A book · selected pages · working draft

Every yoga book is a catalogue of shapes. This one asks eleven questions of every pose — where the weight rests, which way you face, how high you sit, what the spine does, and more — and then asks the same eleven of the moment between two of them. Four still answer. What follows is what it looks like on the page.

The shape of an hour

Every seam in a 60-minute vinyasa class, plotted in order. The class opens loud, runs quiet through the sun salutations and the standing series, and climbs to its loudest seam at the very end.

60-minute vinyasa · heart openers — friction, every seam, in order

Seams that stay within one body position average 0.159 (n=30). Seams that cross into another average 0.556 (n=24) — 3.5× more. That gap is the real mechanism behind the shape above. Full workings, the attribution rule, and the yin exception: the evidence document.
